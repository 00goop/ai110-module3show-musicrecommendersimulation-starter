# Artist Bios (RAG Source #1 — Local Knowledge Base)

This file is the **local retrieval source** for the agent. Each artist below has a short factual bio that the agent looks up after the scoring engine selects top candidates. The agent is instructed to use these bios verbatim where possible to keep responses grounded.

The bios are fictional (this is a simulation catalog), but written in a consistent voice so the agent has something concrete to retrieve.

---

## DJ Sunny
A producer from Miami known for upbeat, festival-ready dance pop. Started uploading bedroom tracks in 2019 and broke out with the single *Sunshine Vibes*, which leans heavily on bright synth stabs and 4-on-the-floor drums. Best paired with sunny weather and group road trips.

## Luna Wave
An R&B singer-songwriter based in Atlanta whose work blends slow-jam vocals with synthwave instrumentation. Her track *Midnight Drive* became a popular late-night listen. Often compared to Kali Uchis and early Frank Ocean.

## Beat Master
An EDM producer who specializes in high-tempo electronic tracks built around heavy synth leads. *Electric Pulse* is the most representative track in his catalog — designed for gym playlists and high-intensity workouts.

## Mellow Mike
An indie singer-songwriter from Portland whose stripped-down acoustic-electric arrangements suit rainy afternoons. *Rainy Thoughts* is widely used in study and focus playlists despite being categorized as "sad" in mood. His work shares DNA with Bon Iver and Phoebe Bridgers.

## DJ Thunder
A festival DJ best known for high-BPM bass-heavy electronic music. *Bass Drop City* is a peak-time floor-filler with a notoriously aggressive drop. Not appropriate for chill playlists despite the dance-pop overlap.

## Sarah Strings
A folk artist whose acoustic-leaning catalog still skews bright and warm. *Acoustic Morning* is a happy folk track sometimes used in coffee shop playlists. Don't confuse with sad-mood folk like *Lonely Highway*.

## The Shadows
A rock band from Detroit specializing in dark, intense alternative rock. *Dark Alley* is their signature track — heavy guitar tone, brooding vocals, and a driving 130 BPM rhythm section. Fans of QOTSA and early Interpol tend to enjoy them.

## Coastal Dreams
A pop duo whose work leans more chill than DJ Sunny's. *Ocean Breeze* sits at a slower tempo with a softer mix — closer to chillwave than dance pop. Useful for users who say "pop" but mean "mellow pop."

## Blaze Band
A hard rock band whose tracks consistently hit high energy and intensity. *Fire Starter* is their most-streamed track and a reliable workout pick if the user prefers rock over electronic.

## Soft Tones
An indie group whose entire catalog lives in low-energy, sad-mood territory. *Lullaby Lane* is, as the name suggests, a sleep-playlist staple. Not appropriate for any energetic recommendation.

## Synth City
A synth-pop / electro group bridging dance music and pop. *Neon Nights* is their crossover track — happy mood with electronic instrumentation, sometimes a useful "near miss" for users asking for happy pop.

## Amber Light
A pop artist whose work is consistently described as "golden hour music" — chill mood, mid-tempo, warm production. *Golden Hour* is the title track of her debut and remains her best-known song.

## Heavy Hitters
A metal-adjacent rock band. *Thunderstorm* is one of the highest-energy tracks in the catalog (0.95 energy, 155 BPM). Recommend only to users who explicitly want intense rock.

## Jazz Cat
An R&B/jazz crossover artist. *Velvet Dreams* is a slow, low-energy chill track suitable for dinner-party playlists. Despite the name, this is not a "dreams"-themed song — the title refers to the velvet vocal texture.

## Party Crew
A pop collective specializing in festival anthems. *Summer Festival* is a high-energy, happy pop track that pairs naturally with *Sunshine Vibes* by DJ Sunny in a "summer pop" playlist.

## Desert Wind
A country artist whose catalog skews melancholic. *Lonely Highway* is a slow, sad country song at 92 BPM — a typical pick for sad-country requests.

## Lo-Fi Larry
An indie/lo-fi producer whose work dominates "study with me" playlists. *Code & Coffee* is his most-streamed track and a near-default pick for chill indie requests.

## Power Track
An electronic producer focused on workout music. *Gym Hero* sits at 0.95 energy and 160 BPM — one of the highest-tempo tracks in the catalog. Recommend for cardio, lift sessions, or any explicit "gym" query.

## Folk Friends
A folk group whose catalog is mostly bright and acoustic. *Campfire Song* is their signature track — happy mood, mid-tempo, suitable for outdoor and group settings. Often confused with sad folk; check mood before recommending.

## Ambient Waves
An ambient/indie producer specializing in low-energy focus music. *Deep Focus* is a 0.25-energy track at 75 BPM, designed for concentration. Pairs well with *Code & Coffee* in a study playlist.
